Questions

Why Telegram and not a regular app?

Because you don't need another app. Telegram is free, fast, and works everywhere. Your pipeline manager should live where your conversations already are, not behind another login.

Can I send it screenshots of texts and emails?

Yes. Send a screenshot of a Zillow notification, a text conversation, an email, or even a photo of a business card. LeadBuddy's AI extracts the name, phone, email, and context automatically.

What if I already have a CRM?

LeadBuddy works alongside your existing CRM or replaces it entirely. Most LOs use it because their "real" CRM is too complex to update on the go. This is the one you'll actually use.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Each user's data is fully isolated with row-level security. Your leads are only visible to you. We never share or sell your data.

How is this different from just using a spreadsheet?

Spreadsheets don't remind you to follow up. They don't extract contact info from screenshots. They don't send you a morning briefing. LeadBuddy is the system that makes sure nothing slips.

Can I use it for my team?

Right now LeadBuddy is built for individual loan officers. Team features (shared pipeline, lead routing) are coming soon.
