FAQ
Questions

Why Telegram and not a regular app?

You don't need another app to learn. Telegram is free, fast, and works on every phone. Your pipeline manager should live where your conversations already are, not behind another login screen.

Can I send screenshots of texts and emails?

Yes. Zillow notifications, text conversations, emails, business cards. LeadBuddy's AI reads the image and extracts the name, phone, email, and context. You just confirm and set a follow-up date.

What if I already have a CRM?

Most LOs use LeadBuddy because their "real" CRM is too complex to update between appointments. Run them side by side, or replace the one you never open. This is the CRM you'll actually use.

Is my data secure?

Yes. 256-bit encryption, row-level data isolation (your leads are invisible to everyone else), and we never share or sell your data. Built with mortgage compliance in mind.

How is this different from a spreadsheet?

Spreadsheets don't remind you to follow up. They don't extract contacts from screenshots. They don't send morning briefings. LeadBuddy is the system that makes sure no lead slips through.

What if I'm not tech-savvy?

If you can send a text message, you can use LeadBuddy. There's no setup screen, no training, no admin panel. You text the bot, it tracks your leads. That's the whole product.
